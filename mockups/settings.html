Shape helper

Optional. Helps you make a custom shape from something in the picture. Cut out this object (click an object in Studio) needs none of this and never sends anything anywhere. Off by default.

Enable the Shape helperShows the Shape helper box under Shapes in the Pro panel, and the Helper tab on phones.
Server keyThe OpenRouter key lives on your server as PIXELSORT_OPENROUTER_KEY. It is never sent to your browser, so it can’t be shown or changed here.
ModelMust accept pictures. A text-only model is refused with a plain message. Checked against OpenRouter’s model list.
Requests per sessionA hard stop for one visit. Each request costs a fraction of a cent.
First-use notice
What is sent: your prompt and a copy of the picture scaled to 512 px on the long edge, with no EXIF, to OpenRouter. Never the original. Nothing is logged by the server. Cancel stops the request. Results are checked by the same validator as a pasted SVG, and nothing is added to your shapes until you press Use it.
