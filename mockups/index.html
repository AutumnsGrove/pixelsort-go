One instrument, five rooms.

Everything below shares one design language — Theater: ink-black stage, warm paper text, one vermilion accent, a serif for voice and a mono for facts. Each page drives a small JS stand-in for the real engine (not the Go/WASM one) so you can watch it sort, spin the direction dial to any angle, tile the picture into shapes, and drop in your own photo.

Things worth trying

Spin the dialIn Studio, tap Direction and drag the dial. While you drag you get an instant live preview at any angle; let go and it replays as the animation. Hold shift to snap to 15°.
Drop your own photoPress N (or “New image”), then drag a picture in, paste one with ⌘V, or paste a link. It sorts your image.
Cut it into shapesPress Tab for Pro, choose hexagons, and give “Kind B” its own direction. Freeze one kind to leave half the picture alone.
Bring your own shapeIn Pro, pick custom, then drop an .svg anywhere (or paste SVG code). A logo, a leaf, a doodle — it tiles the picture and only the shapes sort.
Give a kind its own recipeOpen a region card, switch on Own cutting, and choose cellular, wave or threshold for just that kind. The one-line summary updates as you go.
Protect the sunIn Pro → Protect, tap Freeze the brightest spot (or add an ellipse). Drag it, pull the squares to resize, turn it with the orange dot, or type exact numbers. Add a second shape and switch it to cut a hole.
Roll a presetOpen the preset knob in Studio (or the rail on the phone). Each of the eight built-ins re-rolls its random ranges every time you apply it; nudge a knob and an “edited” tag appears. Save your own, or delete it again.
See what can movePress T to overlay which pixels the current cut and threshold allow to sort, before you run anything. The phone has the same overlay.
Export with a warningOpen Export and pick a size. The dialog plans memory per band, warns when a phone would run out, and offers “Export at 75%”. Watch the banded progress, or cancel part-way.
Punch holes in a shapeIn Pro → custom shapes, start from Ring, Frame or Target. Holes use the even-odd rule, and “tidy this shape” lets you keep pieces, fill small holes or simplify.
Two thousand runsAdd ?runs=2000 to Studio, Archive or the phone. Rows are dated, virtualised and searchable, with a jump rail down the side.
Break things on purposeTry studio.html?used=40 (storage nearly full), ?offline, or ?error=engine (also crash, save, share). Pasting a link containing blocked, notimage, huge or slow triggers the link failures.
Name itClick the name under the picture in Studio, or tap it on the phone (or press F2). The field shows the file name it will export as. Open a run from the strip and rename it, and the new name shows in the Archive and in search. Clear the field to get the automatic name back.
Update itSettings → Updates. Check, then Update now, and watch it ride out the restart. Once a check finds one, a small dot appears on the gear in Studio, on the phone, and beside Settings in the Archive; ?update=available on any page lights it. Add ?update=fail, rollbackfail or none to the Settings address to see the other endings.
Loop and snapPress P, circle something with the brush, then tap Snap — your scribble becomes a shape you can keep editing.

What changed from the first round

BeforeNow
Gallery in the Grove paletteArchive in Theater: serif numerals, frames, the same pill dock for bulk actions.
Phone version in a light “riso” lookPhone Studio in Theater, same controls, six tabs on a bottom sheet.
Direction snapped to 90°A free-form dial (any angle) everywhere: Studio dock, phone sheet, and per-region overrides.
“Freeze the sun” stamped a fixed discMasks are objects: move, resize, rotate, duplicate, delete; union or hole; invert toggle; snap loops into shapes.
Custom shapes were a “soon” tileA shape library: 8 starters, drop or paste an SVG, saved shapes, grid or staggered tiling.
Regions overrode direction / key onlyFoldable region cards with a full per-kind cutting recipe (mode, length, band, rule, sensitivity, unsorted %).
Nothing to help make a custom shapeA Shape helper (design, issue #7): click-to-cut-out, plus an opt-in vision model (OpenRouter) that proposes a shape with a visible transcript, a request cap, and a one-time notice per device.
Regions / masks only on paperA working Pro panel: seven tilings, patterns, per-kind frozen / direction / sort key, feathered masks.
No first-run or share-page design“Bring a picture” intake, and the public share page with its revoked state.
